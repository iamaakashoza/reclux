How it fits together

Four parts. Each with one job.

People mix these up, so here they are side by side. You meet the first three once. After that, you only use the extension.

  1. Setup wizard

    01
    What it is
    The Windows program you download: Reclux-Setup-0.1.0.exe.
    What it does
    Copies Reclux to your PC, finds your browsers, and opens the guide in each one you tick.
    What it can’t do
    Add the extension by itself. Browsers block programs from doing that, for your safety.
    When
    Once. Run it again only to update.

  2. Browser guide

    02
    What it is
    A short page that opens in Chrome, Brave, Edge or Opera.
    What it does
    Four steps with Copy buttons: open the extensions page, turn on Developer mode, Load unpacked, paste the folder.
    What it can’t do
    Click for you. You press three buttons, about 30 seconds.
    When
    Once per browser. Reopen it from the Start menu: “Reclux setup guide”.

  3. Welcome wizard

    03
    What it is
    Four screens inside the Reclux extension: Welcome, Your defaults, How it works, Ready.
    What it does
    Lets you pick what to record, quality, format and sound, and explains each permission.
    What it can’t do
    Record. It only sets things up. Skip it any time.
    When
    Opens by itself after the extension is added. Reopen it with “Setup guide” on the recorder page.

  4. The extension

    04
    What it is
    Reclux itself: the toolbar popup and the recorder page.
    What it does
    Records, pauses, stops and saves your video. Writes it to disk while you record.
    What it can’t do
    Edit or trim videos. Reclux records and saves.
    When
    Every time you record.

Features

Everything a recording needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Screen, window or tab

Pick the whole screen, one app window, or a single browser tab. Your browser always asks first, in its own window, so nothing is captured without your click.

Voice and computer sound

Microphone and system audio, mixed into one track. A Mute button silences your mic without stopping the video.

720p or 1080p, up to 60 fps

Six presets: 720p and 1080p at 30, 50 or 60 frames per second. Reclux shows the frame rate your computer really delivers.

MP4 or WebM

MP4 plays almost everywhere, including Windows’ own player. WebM is there too. The file’s length is always correct, so you can skip through it.

Pause and resume

Paused time is left out of the video, and the timer only counts what is recorded.

Crash-safe, written to disk as you record

Your video is saved in small pieces while you record, so memory stays flat and a crash loses less than a second. If the browser closes, Reclux shows “Found a recording that was never saved” next time, with Recover it or Delete.

Long recordings

Tested for two hours non-stop. At 1h45 Reclux tells you 15 minutes are left of 2 hours; at 2h it says so. It never stops by itself.

Honest warnings

  • Computer struggling: warns when few frames arrive and the CPU is busy, with a one-click switch to 30 fps.
  • Disk running low: warns early, and stops safely before the disk is full, keeping what you recorded.

Keeps your PC awake

Windows won’t sleep or turn off the screen while you record, even when the recorder tab is in the background.

Remembers your choices

Quality, format, sound and what to record are saved. Change them from the toolbar popup without opening the recorder.

Built for everyone

Works fully by keyboard, follows your light or dark setting, grows with large text, and fits small windows.

Using it

A recording, start to saved file.

  1. Press Start recording

    Your browser asks what to share: a tab, a window or the whole screen. Tick “Also share audio” to include the computer’s sound.

  2. 3, 2, 1, and it records

    Switch to other tabs or apps as you like. Reclux keeps recording in the background.

  3. Pause, Resume, Mute or Stop

    The recorder shows the timer, the size so far, and the real frame rate.

  4. Press Save file

    A “Save as” window opens. Pick any folder or drive, such as D: or E:.

    Always press Save file. Until then the video waits in the browser’s private storage on your computer. Clearing the browser’s data would delete it.

Install

Two minutes, once.

Reclux is not in any browser store. The Windows setup wizard copies it to your PC, and a short guide shows you how to add it.

  1. Download and run the setup wizard

    Open Reclux-Setup-0.1.0.exe. No administrator rights are needed.

    Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”, because the file is not code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Tick your browsers and click Finish

    Chrome, Brave, Edge and Opera are found by themselves. Each one you tick opens the browser guide.

  3. Follow the 4 steps in the guide

    Paste the extensions address (for example brave://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and paste the folder the guide gives you.

  4. The welcome wizard opens

    Choose your defaults, then pin Reclux: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin.

Good to know

What Reclux doesn’t do (yet).

  • No editingIt records and saves. Trim in any video editor.
  • No automatic updatesEach new version is a new setup file.
  • A Windows warning on first runThe setup file is not code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Developer mode stays onNeeded for extensions added outside a store. Your browser may remind you about it.
  • Keep the Reclux folderIf %LOCALAPPDATA%\Reclux is deleted, the extension stops working.
  • Speed depends on your computer1080p at 60 fps needs a fast PC. Reclux shows what it really gets.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Is Reclux free?

Yes. There is no account, no trial and nothing to pay.

Where is my recording kept?

On your own computer. While you record, it is written to the browser’s private storage on your drive. When you press Save file, you choose the folder or drive. It is never uploaded.

Does it need the internet?

No. Reclux made zero network requests in our offline test. Only downloading the setup file needs a connection.

Does it keep recording when I switch apps or tabs?

Yes. This was tested with the recorder hidden behind other tabs and apps.

What happens if my browser or PC crashes?

The video is saved in small pieces as you record. Next time you open Reclux, it offers to recover the recording. At most about a second is lost.

Why does Windows warn me about the setup file?

The file is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows does not recognise it yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file with the SHA-256 shown in the download box.

Why do I need Developer mode?

Browsers only add extensions from their own stores automatically. Reclux is not in a store, so you add it once with Load unpacked, which needs Developer mode.

Can I use it in Firefox?

Not from the installer. Firefox only installs add-ons signed by Mozilla. The Firefox version is in the source code for later.

How do I update or uninstall?

To update, run the new setup file and press reload on Reclux in the extensions page. To uninstall, use Windows Settings › Apps › Reclux, then remove Reclux from your browser.

Is the code public?

Yes. You can read all of it on GitHub.
