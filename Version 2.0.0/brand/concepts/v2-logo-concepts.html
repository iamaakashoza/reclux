Reclux V2 · logo concepts

LogoBook test first: every mark must read in plain black at 16 px. Glass is a finish added later, not the idea. Same SVG at every size, no hand-tuning yet.

1 · Mono test (black and white, 128 / 32 / 16 px)

B · Kept Light recommended

your light stays inside your device
  • Says private and offline, with no lock cliché
  • Strongest at 16 px: one cut, one dot
  • Risk: could read as a toggle, so the dot is kept small and away from the end

C · Frame R recommended

a screen-selection corner that is also an R
  • Says screen capture plus the brand letter
  • The R is implied: corner, dot, stroke
  • Risk: corner brackets appear in screenshot tools

A · Lens R

comparison only
  • Close to V1's lens R
  • Risk: an R in a circle reads as ®

D · Iris

comparison only
  • The expected camera answer
  • Risk: every camera app; blurs at 16 px
