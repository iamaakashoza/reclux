Three steps

  1. Add Reclux to your browser from the button above.
  2. A short welcome guide opens. Pick your quality and sound, once.
  3. Press Start recording. When you stop, press Save file and choose any folder or drive.

What you get

  • Record the whole screen, one window, or one tab
  • Microphone and system sound, with a Mute button
  • 720p and 1080p at 30, 50 or 60 frames per second
  • Pause and Resume. Saves as MP4 or WebM
  • Long recordings, written to disk as you go. Tested for 2 hours
  • If the browser crashes, Reclux offers to recover your recording
  • Warns you when the computer is too slow or the disk is nearly full

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and nothing to pay.

Where is my recording kept?

On your own computer. While you record, it is written to the browser's private storage on your drive. When you press Save file, you choose the folder or drive. Nothing is ever uploaded.

Does it need the internet?

No. Reclux makes no network requests at all.

Does it work on iPad, iPhone or Android?

Not yet. Those browsers do not allow this kind of extension to record the screen. Reclux is for desktop computers. It is tested on Windows. Mac and Linux should work in the same browsers, but are not tested yet.

Does it edit videos?

No. Reclux records and saves. It does not trim or edit.
