Reclux Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

On this page

What Reclux handles What Reclux does not do Your permission, every time Why Reclux asks for its permissions Deleting your data Children Changes Contact

Short version: Reclux does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What Reclux handles

WhatWhere it stays
The picture of your screen, a window or a tab, and the microphone or system sound you choose to recordWritten to your browser's private storage on your own computer, then to the folder you pick when you press Save file
Your choices (what to record, quality, format, microphone on or off)Saved in your browser's local storage on your own computer
Computer load (CPU use) and frames per secondRead while recording, only to show a warning. Not stored, not sent
Free disk spaceRead while recording, only to show a warning. Not stored, not sent

What Reclux does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No uploading of recordings or any other data
  • No analytics, ads, tracking or cookies
  • No third-party services
  • No network requests of any kind. It works with the internet switched off

Your permission, every time

Your browser asks you before Reclux can capture the screen, a window, a tab or the microphone. Reclux only records while you have started a recording.

Why Reclux asks for its permissions

PermissionReason
downloadsShows the "Save as" window, so you can choose the folder or drive for the finished recording
unlimitedStorageLets a long recording be written to the browser's private storage while you record
system.cpuReads how busy the computer is, only to warn you that it is too slow to keep up (Chrome, Brave, Edge and Opera)
powerKeeps the computer awake while a recording is running (Chrome, Brave, Edge and Opera)

Deleting your data

  • Unsaved recordings: press Discard or Delete in Reclux.
  • Saved files: they are normal files in the folder you chose. Delete them like any other file.
  • Settings: removed when you uninstall Reclux or clear the extension's data.

Children

Reclux is a general tool. It does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published at this address.
