# Reclux Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

**Short version: Reclux does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your device.**

## What Reclux handles
| What | Where it stays |
|---|---|
| Screen, window or tab picture, and microphone or system sound you choose to record | Written to your browser's private storage on your own computer, then to the folder you pick when you press **Save file** |
| Your choices (what to record, quality, format, mic on/off) | Saved in your browser's local storage on your own computer |
| Computer load (CPU use) and frames per second | Read while recording, only to show a warning. Not stored, not sent |
| Free disk space | Read while recording, only to show a warning. Not stored, not sent |

## What Reclux does not do
- No account or sign-in
- No uploading of recordings or any other data
- No analytics, ads, tracking or cookies
- No third-party services
- No network requests of any kind. It works with the internet off

## Your permission, every time
Your browser asks you before Reclux can capture the screen, a window, a tab or the microphone. Reclux only records while you have started a recording.

## Deleting your data
- **Unsaved recordings:** press **Discard** or **Delete** in Reclux.
- **Saved files:** they are normal files in the folder you chose. Delete them like any file.
- **Settings:** removed when you uninstall Reclux or clear the extension's site data.

## Children
Reclux is a general tool and does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
